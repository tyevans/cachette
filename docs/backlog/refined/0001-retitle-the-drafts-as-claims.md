## Why

A title that states a claim predicts a more stable record than a title that
names a topic. Five of the six drafts carry topic titles.

## Impact review

**Governed by.** The record scope rule. It requires a title to state the
decision the record makes.

**Changes.** The six drafts, their registry rows, and any citation of them.
A title change is not a decision change, so no record is superseded.

**Creates.** None.

**Blockers.** None.

**Precedent.** FND-034 records the measurement behind this.

## Done when

- Each of the six titles states the claim its record makes.
- The registry rows match the file titles.
- The record check passes, including title and number agreement.
- No citation of a record is left dangling.

Filled in on completion.
