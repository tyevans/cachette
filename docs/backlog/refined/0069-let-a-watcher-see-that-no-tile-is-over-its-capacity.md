The product record for the first renderable example asks for two things about
capacity. No tile holds more entities than its capacity allows, and a viewer
can see that this holds. The audit met neither.

The panel reports the mean number of units on an open tile of the region under
the crosshair. A mean of one unit a tile is consistent with one tile holding
four hundred. The panel has no maximum, no count of tiles at capacity, and no
colour for a tile that is over.

The audit spawned four hundred soldiers onto one tile of ordinary ground and
drew the frame. The viewer painted every one of them on that tile and said
nothing.

The first half of the statement is an open choice, and the register holds it
with the assumption the engine follows.[^1] This item is the second half: the
watcher must be able to see the answer, whichever way the choice goes.

Refine this with the decision that governs the spawn.
