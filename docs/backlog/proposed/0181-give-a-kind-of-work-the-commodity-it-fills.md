## Why

A site opens positions of each kind of work in proportion to what it lacks of
each. What it lacks is what it wants of a kind, less what it holds of the
commodity that the kind fills.

**The map from a kind of work to a commodity carries no information today.**
The store of a site holds one commodity, so every kind of work maps onto that
one entry. A site therefore holds the same amount against every kind, and the
number of positions of each kind follows the preference alone. A place that
runs out of one thing and holds plenty of another cannot tell the two apart,
because it has only one thing.

This is a placeholder that reads as a rule. A contributor who opens the table
sees a map and assumes it means something.

## What the work does

1. Give the store of a site more than one commodity, or state why it holds
   one.
2. Map each kind of work onto the commodity it fills.
3. Give the number of positions of a kind a test that changes one commodity
   and not another, and asserts that only one kind of position moves.
